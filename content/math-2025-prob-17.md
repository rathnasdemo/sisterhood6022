*Adapted from the video [Math 2025 prob 17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ag1yuaG4LtY)*

Here's the problem. In the land of Marovia, there are three cities: A, B and C. There are 100 people in A, 120 people in B and 160 people in C. Everyone works in one of the three cities, and a person may work in the same city where they live.

In the figure that comes with the problem, an arrow pointing from one city to another is labeled with the fraction of the people living in the first city who work in the second city. For example, 1/4 of the people who live in A work in B.

The question: how many people work in A?

## Turning the fractions into numbers

*[Watch from 1:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ag1yuaG4LtY&t=83s)*

We know that there are 100 people in A, 120 people in B and 160 people in C.

Let's start with A. How many people go out of A to work? 1/4 and 1/5 are both very compatible with 100:

- 1/4 of 100 is 25.
- 1/5 of 100 is 20.

Likewise with B:

- 1/3 of 120 is 40.
- 1/6 of 120 is 20.

And with C:

- 1/8 of 160 is 20.
- 1/10 of 160 is 16.

Now that we've changed all the fractions into numbers, this can be a lot easier.

## Counting everyone who works in A

*[Watch from 2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ag1yuaG4LtY&t=166s)*

We don't need to solve for any of the others, B or C, because they're only asking about A. To find that, we look at the arrows pointing toward A: 40 people from B and 20 people from C.

We also need to find how many people go out of A to work, so we know how many people live in A and work in A. That's 100 - 25 - 20, which equals 55. So there are 55 people who live in A and work in A.

We need to add that to the 40 people from B who work in A and the 20 people from C who work in A. That's 60 + 55, which is 115.

So 115 people work in A, and that is our answer: D, 115.
