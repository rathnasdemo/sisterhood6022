*Adapted from the video [Math SAT STD Problem - 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LdpQ6AHdC3E)*

Here's the problem. A bus travels the 36 miles from Framingham to Worcester in 40 minutes. A car traveling from Worcester to Framingham drives 1.5 times as fast as the bus. If the car and the bus start moving toward each other at the same time, after how many minutes will they meet?

## Setting up what we know

*[Watch from 0:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LdpQ6AHdC3E&t=22s)*

The distance is clear: 36 miles. So is the time: 40 minutes.

Now, what about the speed? That's unknown, so we'll label it x.

The car is obviously driving the same distance, just in the opposite direction, and it's 1.5 times as fast as the bus. The bus is x, so the car is 1.5 × x.

## The formula for when they meet

*[Watch from 1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LdpQ6AHdC3E&t=60s)*

Here's where a crucial formula peeks in. We already know that speed times time equals distance, and that can be written in other ways too, like distance divided by time equals speed.

Now let's make that a little more specific for this problem. The distance divided by the bus speed plus the car speed will tell us when they meet.

## Finding the bus and car speeds

*[Watch from 1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LdpQ6AHdC3E&t=117s)*

The bus speed is x, and we can easily solve for it using the same formula: speed times time equals distance. That lets us say that 36 / 40 equals x. And 36 / 40 can be written as 9 / 10, aka 0.9.

Next, 0.9 × 1.5. Everyone loves multiplying decimals, I know. Working out 1.5 × 0.9 gives 1.35.

So the bus goes 0.9 per minute (not per hour, which is why the numbers are so small), and the car goes 1.35.

## Dividing the distance by the combined speed

*[Watch from 3:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LdpQ6AHdC3E&t=190s)*

Now 0.9 + 1.35 equals 2.25.

With that in hand, we take the distance, which is 36, and divide it by 2.25. And 36 / 2.25 is the same as 3,600 divided by 225.

Time for some long division. 225 goes into 360 once, quite obviously, and that leaves us 135. We bring down the zero and try a six. It fits in perfectly, with nothing left over.

## The answer

*[Watch from 5:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LdpQ6AHdC3E&t=301s)*

So the answer is 16. The bus and the car will take 16 minutes to meet.
