*Adapted from the video [Our New Home](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sv_RRTMrn2w)*

November 8, 2021. San Ramon, California. This is our little ADU.

Today was our housewarming, so I'll give you a quick rundown of what we've done so far. This is our little entrance. The ADU has around 702 square feet of space.

## The living room and kitchen

*[Watch from 0:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sv_RRTMrn2w&t=22s)*

Let's go inside and see what we've done. As soon as you enter, on the right-hand side is our living room. It has a few recessed lights and a mini split. This is a three-way split, with the compressor on the other side of the wall. One head is in the living room, one is in the bedroom and another is in the office room. There's also a little pantry.

On the left-hand side we have our kitchen. We installed all these cabinets a couple of weeks ago, and they came out beautiful. All the appliances are new Samsung. We got them as a package, all four together, and got a pretty decent discount on them as well.

We have a skylight here which opens up to the roof, so during the daytime we usually wouldn't need the lights on. The skylight would be more than enough. And we have a window as well.

## The office room and half bath

*[Watch from 1:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sv_RRTMrn2w&t=83s)*

Let's enter our office room. We have a fan, a recessed light, one of the split heads and a 4x4 window. There's no closet on this side, and that's why it's called an office room. Officially we are allowed to build only a one-bedroom ADU, I suppose, so our architects recommended doing it this way.

This door leads us to a half bath. There's a beautiful vanity light, and it doesn't need a separate vanity light: the mirror comes with the LED light, and it has three different shades of white. The mirror also has a feature for fog, but we don't have a shower in here, so we might not use that feature in this restroom.

There's a vanity, a skylight here as well up on the roof, and an exhaust. The potty is on the left-hand side. This one has a motion sensor light, so it came on by itself, and we have the bidet installed as well.

## The master bedroom

*[Watch from 2:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sv_RRTMrn2w&t=157s)*

Going back to the living room and into the master bedroom. This one is 16 by 11, I think, with about four feet less on one side for the restroom. There's a fan, a few recessed lights and the other mini split head.

We have a full-length closet and a tiny closet. Right now we're using the tiny one as an entrance to the attic. We haven't planned what we're going to do with that space yet. In itself it's a pretty decent size for a closet. It's still in the works. We have a 4x4 window here as well, so there's enough lighting.

## The full bathroom

*[Watch from 3:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sv_RRTMrn2w&t=202s)*

This is our restroom, with a vanity and the same mirror with the LED lights, so no separate vanity lights are needed. There's a recessed light here as well and the exhaust.

They just installed these shower doors, and I just love them. They're super smooth to use and beautiful, and they save a lot of space because there's no space needed for swinging swing doors.

We got these beautiful showers from Amazon. There are two different heads: one can be height adjusted, and one is static, though I think it can be tilted a little bit. Both can be switched on at the same time, and I love that feature. There are tiles on all three sides of the wall.

## The laundry space and backyard

*[Watch from 4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sv_RRTMrn2w&t=259s)*

Off to the living room again, making a U-turn, this is our little laundry space.

We kind of made a bad decision getting a full-fledged laundry. We should have got a laundry tower or something with a slimmer profile. This is a full-fledged, standard-size washer and dryer, so it's pretty tight here.

We'll see, we might change it a little bit. The plan was to use this space for something like a shelf, but I'm not sure what we're going to do. We have a little more room than we need on one side, so we might be able to move the machines toward the other wall a little bit, and then we'll have enough space for a shelf there.

Opening this door takes us to the backyard. It's a pretty decent-sized backyard, not bad for an ADU.

That's pretty much what we have. Thank you!
